Five easy steps

What's inside

1. Lectio — Hit the Text!

Read the daily passage. Pick a verse that makes you think "mic-drop."

2. Meditatio — How's It Hit You?

Think about the word. How does it relate to your friends, school, or what's on your mind?

3. Oratio — Real Talk with God

Just be real. Tell God your honest thoughts, fears, or questions.

4. Contemplatio — Find Your Calm

Chill out. Turn down the noise. What do you feel God saying in your heart?

5. Actio — One Small Win

What's one thing you can do today based on this prayer? Keep it practical.

Current Vibe check-in

A quick mood tracker at the top of every page — a small way to notice how you're really doing.
